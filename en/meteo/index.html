Weather and sea conditions in Ksar Sghir

Ksar Sghir sits on the southern shore of the Strait of Gibraltar, where the weather depends heavily on the easterly Levante and the westerly Poniente. With the Levante, waves on the beach stay normal; with the Poniente, they get bigger and you need to take more care, especially with children. In winter the sea is rough on all the area’s beaches. Check this page before heading to the beach or out fishing.
