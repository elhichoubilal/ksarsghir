Weather and sea conditions in Ksar Sghir

Ksar Sghir sits on the southern shore of the Strait of Gibraltar, where the weather depends heavily on the easterly Levante and the westerly Poniente. Strong Levante days bring waves to the beach, while Poniente days are often calmer for swimming.
