📍 Sidi Kankouch 2 Beach — The Quieter Twin

Sidi Kankouch 2 lies 5 km from its neighbor (20 km from Ksar Sghir). Quieter and less crowded, it is the smart choice for those wanting similar services in a more peaceful atmosphere.

🌊 Features

🤫
Quieter
Less crowded — perfect for those seeking real relaxation.
🛡️
Seasonal supervision
July-August presence for swimmer safety.
📸
More scenic
The setting with surrounding hills offers superior visual beauty.
🌊
Excellent waters
Same quality strait water with moderate waves.

💡 Tips for Choosing

  • 🤔
    Want restaurants and all services? Choose SK1. Prefer quiet with basic facilities? Choose SK2.
  • 🧭
    Tell the driver clearly "Sidi Kankouch 2" to avoid the wrong stop.
