📍 Zahara / Hatba Beach — Closest and Calmest

Zahara / Hatba Beach is just 3 km west — the easiest to reach. Its geographic setting protects it from northeast winds, making waves calmer than the main beach.

🌊 Zahara's Advantages

📍
Exceptionally close
Only 3 km — walkable in mild season or taxi for under 5 dirhams.
🌊
Child-friendly waves
Natural geographic protection makes waters ideal for safe family swimming.
🛡️
Seasonal supervision
July-August presence for safety.
💰
Economical
Proximity reduces costs — ideal for frequent daily visits.
🌅
Mountain sunset
Sunset behind western mountains differs from open beaches — authentic scene.
🌿
Nearby wild nature
Surrounding slopes teem with wild plants enriching the visit.

🏪 Services

⚠️ Limited services — some ambulant vendors in summer. Better to bring your own food and water.
