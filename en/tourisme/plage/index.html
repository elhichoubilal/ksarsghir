Ksar Sghir Beach

Ksar Sghir's main beach — golden sand and clear waters on the Strait of Gibraltar. Supervised, free, ideal for families.

📍 Location and Geographic Importance

Ksar Sghir Beach extends along the city's main seafront in a geographically significant position on the southern shore of the Strait of Gibraltar. Located in the heart of the coastal city of Ksar Sghir in the Fahs-Anjra province of the Tangier-Tétouan-Al Hoceima region, it lies about thirty-five kilometers east of Tangier along the coastal N16 highway.

What gives this beach its exceptional character is its direct view of the Strait of Gibraltar — that strategic waterway separating the African and European continents and connecting the Atlantic Ocean to the Mediterranean Sea. On clear days, visitors can see with the naked eye the Spanish coast and the famous Rock of Gibraltar just fourteen kilometers away across the blue waters — a sight that makes every visit a unique and unforgettable visual and geographical experience.

The beach is notably situated in the very heart of the city, making it accessible to all residents and visitors without needing to travel far. This gives it a vibrant social character, making it a natural meeting point for families, young people, and children throughout the summer season.

🏖️ Beach Character and Natural Features

Ksar Sghir Beach is made up of fine golden sand that lends the place a serene beauty. The waters are characterized by a high degree of clarity and purity, thanks to the strong currents of the Strait of Gibraltar that continuously renew and cleanse the water. This makes the swimming water quality significantly better than beaches in enclosed bays or landlocked lagoons.

Water temperature ranges from moderate in spring and autumn to relatively warm in summer, but the strait's currents always provide a distinctive freshness that makes swimming a real pleasure even on the hottest days of July and August. Depth increases gradually, providing a safe shallow zone for children while offering enough space for free swimming for adults in the deeper sections.

In the early morning hours, when the sea is calm and free of visitors, gentle waves break on the golden sand in a scene of absolute tranquility, making the beach an ideal place for contemplation, morning swimming, and relaxation away from daily life. By evening, it transforms into a lively social space where families gather in a safe and joyful atmosphere.

🛡️ Facilities and Available Services

🛡️
Civil Protection
Permanent presence of Moroccan Civil Protection throughout the summer season, with a dedicated watchtower and rescue boats ready for immediate intervention. This presence ensures swimmer safety and gives families complete peace of mind.
🚿
Showers and Toilets
Basic sanitation facilities including rinse showers after swimming and public toilets, making the beach day comfortable from start to finish.
🍽️
Restaurants and Kiosks
Several seasonal restaurants and kiosks along the beach offering fresh grilled fish, seafood, and cold drinks.
🛒
Shops
Small stores selling beach essentials including sunscreen, children's toys, towels, and swimwear.
🅿️
Parking
Parking spaces near the beach allow visitors arriving by car to park safely and access the beach on foot within minutes.
🏥
First Aid
First aid station provided by Civil Protection, equipped to handle minor emergencies and situations requiring immediate medical attention.

💡 Practical Tips

  • ⏰
    Golden timing: Arrive before 10am to get the best spots near the water, especially on busy summer days.
  • 🌞
    Sun protection: Use sunscreen with SPF 50 minimum — the strait's rays are stronger than you expect.
  • 💧
    Hydration: Bring enough water or purchase from nearby shops.
  • 🏊
    Safe swimming: Stay in Civil Protection supervised zones and respect warning flags.

🚌 How to Get There

  • 🚌
    Public Bus: Lines I3 and I12 from Tangier train station — approximately 45-minute ride at very low cost.
  • 🚕
    Grand Taxi: From Tangier station square, regular shared taxis to Ksar Sghir.
  • 🚗
    Private Car: National road N16 from Tangier — 35 to 40 minutes along a scenic coastal route.
📍 GPS Coordinates: 35.6897° N, 5.5597° W. Type "Ksar Sghir Beach" in Google Maps for direct access.
