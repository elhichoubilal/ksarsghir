📍 Oued Alian Beach — The Wide Quiet Shore

Oued Alian Beach stretches twelve kilometers east of Ksar Sghir. Its wide golden sands can accommodate large numbers of visitors even at peak season — a rare advantage on northern Morocco's crowded shores.

🌊 Beach Features

🏖️
Very wide beach
Long sandy stretch offering space for everyone.
👨‍👩‍👧
Family-friendly
Moderate waters and soft sand for young children.
🛡️
Seasonal supervision
Present in July-August. Check before swimming far.
🌅
Panoramic view
See Tanger Med port and Spanish coast on clear days.
🐟
Excellent fishing
Rich strait waters make it a top rod-fishing spot.

🏪 Available Services

⚠️ No restaurants or showers — bring your own food and water.

🎯 Activities

🏊
Swimming

🐟

💡 Practical Tips

  • ⏰
    Arrive early in summer — parking fills up quickly.
  • 🍱
    Bring food and water — no services on the beach.

🚌 How to Get There

  • 🚌
    Bus I3/I12 from Tangier.
  • 🚗
    Coastal road N16.
  • 🚕
    Taxi from Ksar Sghir.
