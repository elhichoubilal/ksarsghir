⚓ Traditional Fishing Port — The Beating Heart of Ksar Sghir

The traditional fishing port of Ksar Sghir, 0.5 km from the main beach, is a daily theater where the city's real life unfolds. From dawn when boats return laden to mid-afternoon when fishermen prepare for the night, the port lives a fascinating daily rhythm.

🐟 The Fish Auction — Tradition, Economy and Life

The daily auction is what distinguishes the port: the auctioneer announces species and quantities aloud. Traditional method combining economic efficiency and social warmth. Varied strait fish — fresh from sea to hand within hours.

⚓ What Makes the Port Unique

⚓
Living port
Unlike tourist-museum ports — Ksar Sghir port fulfills its real function every day.
🐟
Exceptionally fresh fish
From sea to hand within hours — freshness incomparable anywhere.
📸
Photo mine
Boat colors, fishermen in action, fish on ice — inexhaustible visual material.
🌅
Sunset departure scene
Watching boats light up one by one and sail off — unforgettable poetic sight.
🤝
Authentic social space
Tight social fabric: fishermen, traders, residents — real daily human exchanges.
🍽️
From port to your plate
Buy from fisherman, a nearby restaurant will cook it Moroccan-style.
🌅 Rare night experience: Sitting in the evening watching boats depart one by one toward the star-lit horizon — a deeply human experience.

📍 Access

  • 🚶
    Walking: 500 m from main beach — 6 minutes along the seafront.
  • 🚶
    From center: in the heart of the city — any local knows it.
📍 GPS: 35.6895° N, 5.5590° W.
