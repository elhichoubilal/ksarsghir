📍 Dahdaha Beach — Where Mountains Meet the Sea

Dahdaha Beach occupies a dramatic geographic position where green mountain chains descend toward the strait's waters, 10 km east. Its relative access difficulty maintains rare natural calm even in peak season.

🏔️ What Sets Dahdaha Apart

🏔️
Enchanting mountain setting
Green mountains create an unmatched natural scene.
🌊
Calm, clear waters
The mountain-protected bay offers calmer waters — ideal for children.
🤫
Exceptional tranquility
Difficult access keeps the beach quiet even at peak July-August.
🌅
Unforgettable sunset
Colors shift from blue to gold to orange — a rare chromatic spectacle.
📸
Exceptional photography
Mountains, sea and sky together — every shot here is a work of art.
🏕️
Nature camping
Night between mountains and sea under stars — an unmatched experience.

🏪 Services

⚠️ No showers or restaurants. Seasonal Civil Protection only. Bring everything. Mountain road difficult in rain.
