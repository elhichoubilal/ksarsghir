📍 Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach — The Complete Beach

Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach is among the best-equipped beaches in the region, 25 km east of Ksar Sghir near Tanger Med. The ideal choice for families wanting all amenities without prior preparation.

🌊 Features

🚿
Full showers & toilets
Complete sanitation facilities — a major advantage.
🛡️
Permanent Civil Protection
Continuous presence July-August giving families total peace of mind.
🍽️
Seasonal restaurants
Restaurants serving fresh fish and seafood.
🅿️
Dedicated parking
Dedicated parking space close to the beach.
👨‍👩‍👧
Perfect for families
Complete combination of safety, facilities and food.
✅ Best family choice: Sidi Kankouch 1 is ideal for a complete beach day with no hassles.
