Practical tips (unofficial)

  • ⏰ Arrive early, especially by car and in summer: customs and border checks take time.
  • 🌬️ Watch the Levante: strong easterly winds can delay or cancel sailings. See today’s sea.
  • 🧳 Documents: a valid passport, a visa if you need one, and your vehicle papers.
  • ☀️ Operation Marhaba: since summer 2025 a confirmed ticket (billet ferme) is compulsory during the operation, which has made crossings smoother. Book early.
  • 🎫 Tickets: book on the company’s official site or through a trusted agency and keep your confirmation.

To learn more about the port and its figures, read Tanger Med Port.

FAQ

How long is the Tanger Med – Algeciras crossing?

About an hour and a half, depending on the ship and weather.

Where can I find up-to-date times?

On the live board above, on the official Tanger Med Passagers site and with your ferry company.

Does the Levante cancel sailings?

On very windy days sailings may be delayed or cancelled. Check with the company before setting off.

Do Tarifa ferries leave from Tanger Med?

No, the Tarifa route usually leaves from Tanger Ville port. From Tanger Med the main route is Algeciras.
