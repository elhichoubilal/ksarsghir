The 3 best family beaches in Ksar Sghir

The 3 best family beaches in Ksar Sghir: Sidi Kankouch 1, the town beach and Oued Alian. Lifeguards, facilities, crowds and tips for kids.

Updated: 9 October 2026

In this article

  1. The three beaches
  2. Quick comparison
  3. Tips for families
  4. Other beaches worth a visit
⚡ Quick answer
  1. Sidi Kankouch 1: the best equipped (showers, restaurants, lifeguards in season).
  2. Ksar Sghir beach: right in town, easy to reach and close to everything.
  3. Oued Alian: very wide, with room for kids even at peak season.

We picked these beaches on what matters most to families: safety and lifeguards, facilities, easy access and space for children. The information is based on the Ksar Sghir guide pages and on a local’s experience.

The three beaches

1. Sidi Kankouch 1 beach

⭐ Best if you want everything in one place

  • 🛡️ Lifeguards in July–August
  • 🚿 Showers and toilets
  • 🍽️ Seasonal restaurants
  • 🅿️ Parking
  • 👥 Busy at peak times

One of the best-equipped beaches in the area, on the coast road west of Ksar Sghir, towards Tangier. You can spend the whole day without packing food or worrying about showers.

All about Sidi Kankouch 1 →

2. Ksar Sghir beach

🏙️ The closest and easiest

  • 🛡️ Lifeguards in summer
  • 💰 Free
  • 🚶 In the town centre
  • 👥 Busy in August

The town’s main beach, with golden sand and a direct view of the Strait and the Spanish coast on clear days. Cafés and shops are nearby, so no long trips with the kids.

All about Ksar Sghir beach →

3. Oued Alian beach

🏖️ For those who want space

  • 🛡️ Lifeguards on and off
  • 📏 Very wide
  • 🍽️ No restaurants or showers

A long stretch of sand that takes big crowds even in high season, with moderate water and soft sand suited to young children. No facilities, so bring food and water.

⚠️ Note: lifeguards and facilities are seasonal (usually July–August) and change from year to year. Always follow the flags and lifeguards, and avoid swimming on strong Levante days. Check today’s sea conditions before you go.

Tips for families

  • Arrive early in August: easier parking and gentler sun for the kids.
  • Bring shade and plenty of water, especially to beaches without facilities.
  • The easterly Levante builds waves on the beach; Poniente (westerly) days are usually calmer.
  • Agree on a clear meeting point with the kids near the lifeguard post.

Other beaches worth a visit

  • Dalia beach: calm, family atmosphere.
  • Zahara / Hatba: usually gentle waves.
  • Sidi Kankouch 2: same feel, fewer people.
