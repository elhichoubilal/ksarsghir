Top 3 family beaches in Ksar Sghir

The top 3 family beaches in Ksar Sghir: Ksar Sghir Beach, Sidi Kankouch 1 and Dalia (Blue Flag). Facilities, activities and currents compared, with tips for kids.

Updated: 16 October 2026

In this article

  1. 🏖️ The three beaches
  2. 📊 Quick comparison
  3. 👨‍👩‍👧 Family tips
  4. 🌊 More family beaches
⚡ Quick answer
  1. Ksar Sghir Beach: weak currents, free parasols and kids’ games, right in the heart of the area.
  2. Sidi Kankouch 1: weak currents, free parasols, showers and an activity platform.
  3. Dalia: 11-time Blue Flag, activity platform and a space for people with special needs.

We chose these beaches based on what matters most to families: calm sea, lifeguards, facilities, activities for kids and easy access. Every beach in the area has Civil Protection lifeguards from June to September, but these three bring together the most facilities in one place.

🏖️ The three beaches

1. Ksar Sghir Beach

⭐ Best for families: calm sea and free facilities

  • 🌊 Weak currents
  • ⛱️ Free parasols
  • 🛝 Free kids’ games
  • 🚻 Toilets
  • 🛟 Rescue boat

The area’s main beach, also known as Ksar El Majaz beach. Its currents are weak, and in summer it has free parasols and kids’ games, toilets and a rescue boat, plus activities run by a local association. Restaurants and shops are in the centres of Ksar Sghir and Ksar El Majaz; buses I12 and I3 come here from Tangier.

2. Sidi Kankouch 1 Beach

🎨 For families who want activities and facilities

  • 🌊 Weak currents
  • ⛱️ Free parasols
  • 🚿 Showers and toilets
  • 🎨 Activity platform
  • 🅿️ Paid parking

A wide sandy beach west of Ksar Sghir towards Tangier, about 25 km from Ksar Sghir. Weak currents, free parasols, showers, toilets, and seasonal kiosks and restaurants. The activity platform runs competitions, drawing workshops and a radio, with a first-aid point.

All about Sidi Kankouch 1 →

3. Dalia Beach

🏳️ Blue Flag and the most activities

  • 🏳️ Blue Flag 11 times
  • 🎨 Activity platform
  • ♿ Special-needs space
  • ⛵ Sailing school
  • 🟡 Swim inside the yellow buoys

Awarded the Blue Flag 11 times, most recently in 2026. It has an activity platform for kids (games, drawing workshop, football, beach volleyball, pétanque, a beach library), a space for people with special needs with helpers, and a sailing school open all year. Currents are stronger than at Ksar Sghir, so swim inside the area marked by the yellow buoys. About 8 km east of Ksar Sghir, served by bus I3.

⚠️ Lifeguards and facilities are seasonal, June to September. Always follow the flags and the Civil Protection’s instructions, and avoid swimming in winter.

👨‍👩‍👧 Family tips

  • 🌅
    Go in the morning, especially at weekends in August: easier parking and gentler sun for the kids.
  • 📍
    Agree on a meeting point with the children, such as the lifeguard post.
  • 🟡
    Swim inside the yellow buoys in the guarded zone.
  • 🌬️
    With the Levante, waves stay normal; with the Poniente, they get bigger and you need more care. Check Sea today before you go.

🌊 More family beaches

  • Oued Alian: a large sandy beach with weak currents, showers and toilets.
  • Dahdaha and Zrara: true family coves between hills and sea.
  • Zahara / Hatba: a quiet cove 3 km from Ksar Sghir.
  • Sidi Kankouch 2: a quiet cove with parasols and toilets, next to Sidi Kankouch 1.
